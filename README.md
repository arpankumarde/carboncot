# CarbonCot

CarbonCot is a prototype carbon credit marketplace designed to make carbon offsetting simple, transparent, and accessible. It provides individuals and businesses with a platform to explore verified carbon projects, purchase credits, and retire them with ease. The platform emphasizes credibility and user-friendliness, showcasing how carbon markets can be streamlined for broader adoption.

## Purpose

The goal of CarbonCot is to demonstrate the concept of a carbon credit marketplace. It highlights how users can offset their carbon footprints by supporting verified projects that align with global sustainability goals. By focusing on transparency and simplicity, CarbonCot aims to educate users about the potential of carbon credits in combating climate change.

## Key Features

### 1. Browse Carbon Projects

- Explore a variety of carbon projects categorized by:
  - **Country**: Filter projects based on their geographic location.
  - **Project Type**: Forestry, Renewable Energy, Infrastructure, etc.
  - **Certification**: Verra, Gold Standard, and other recognized standards.
  - **SDG Goals**: Projects aligned with specific Sustainable Development Goals.
  - **Price Range**: Find projects that fit your budget.

### 2. Project Details

- Each project listing provides comprehensive information, including:
  - Project name, type, and location.
  - Certification details (e.g., Verra, Gold Standard).
  - Vintage year and credits available.
  - Price per credit and SDG goals supported.
  - Links to registry or verification pages.

### 3. Retirement Process

- Retire carbon credits in a few simple steps:
  1. Select a project and specify the number of credits to retire.
  2. Input optional details like beneficiary name and retirement purpose.
  3. Complete a dummy payment step (no real transactions).
  4. Instantly generate a retirement certificate.

### 4. Digital Retirement Certificates

- Generate and download certificates that include:
  - Project details (name, location, certification).
  - Retirement amount (tons of CO₂e offset).
  - Beneficiary name and retirement purpose.
  - Unique certificate ID and issue date.
- Certificates are shareable on social media to promote climate action.

### 5. Lightweight Prototype

- CarbonCot is a front-end-focused prototype designed for demonstration purposes. It uses pre-filled data to simulate the browsing, purchasing, and retirement processes without real payments or backend integrations.

## Why CarbonCot?

CarbonCot addresses the challenges of navigating and verifying carbon credits online. By providing a transparent and intuitive platform, it empowers users to make informed decisions about their carbon offsetting efforts. Whether you're an individual looking to offset personal emissions or a business aiming to meet ESG goals, CarbonCot offers a simple and credible solution.
